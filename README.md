Entry for [Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment
strange and new. Running `main.py` writes that sentence (122 characters) to
`/output/entry.txt`, where the judge reads it.

> The washing machine finishes its spin and the house goes suddenly quiet,
> as if a small storm had just apologized and left.
